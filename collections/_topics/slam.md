The overall objective in this research area is to enable mixed-capability UAS–UGV teams to maintain useful estimates of their environment and make effective navigation decisions despite uncertain localization, unreliable contributors, and exposure constraints. To that end, we develop scalar field mapping techniques, active sensing techniques, fast and parallel occupancy grid mapping techniques, and distributed mapping techniques.
